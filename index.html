Privacy Policy

LiftKlub ("we", "our", or "us") provides a club management, turnstile admission, and athletic tracking platform. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect user data when using the LiftKlub mobile application across iOS and Android.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Data: We collect your mobile phone number and name for authentication, user identification, and membership verification.
  • Fitness & Attendance Records: We record turnstile check-in timestamps, private workout journal logs, and gym class reservations.
  • Camera Data: The application requests access to your device camera strictly for real-time turnstile dynamic QR code barcode recognition. No video streams, live frames, or photographs are recorded or transmitted off your device during scanning.
  • Optional Gallery Access: If you choose to upload a profile avatar or record workout evidence, we access the specific photo selected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage active gym access passes and anti-passback turnstile verification.
  • To facilitate front-desk till reconciliation and cash concessions transactions.
  • To isolate private ladies-only schedules and confidential vault data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data. Under Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play User Data policies, users can request full account deletion directly within the LiftKlub mobile app:

  • Navigate to the Athlete Profile Hub in the app.
  • Tap "Delete My Account" and confirm the prompt.
  • Your active membership passes will be cancelled immediately, and personal profile identifiers (name, phone number, avatar) will be permanently anonymized. Immutable financial accounting ledgers remain sealed for required business audit trails.
